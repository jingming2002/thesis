# Homologue identification and phylogenetic analysis

Fhs BLAST used five high-confidence proteins; trees contained five or 52 viral candidates (5 high, 2 medium, 45 low). Viral confidence and ORF integrity were separate. Seven other sets were CD-HIT clustered (4.8.1; 70% identity, both coverages ≥80%; word length 4 for FolD/NrdG, 5 otherwise). Trees retained one protein per named PhaGCN3 family and up to ten farthest-first unclassified representatives.

BLASTP searched local RefSeq (Bacteria/Archaea; 20 targets/query). Search E-values were 0.001 for Fhs/five rebuilt families and 1 × 10−5 for FolD/NrdG; hits required bit score ≥50 and E-value ≤0.001. Seven families used one best hit per selected viral protein; Fhs retained up to five accessions/query. Accessions were merged and full-length sequences retrieved; identity/coverage filters and NCBI CD-HIT were not used. Version 2.16.0+ is recorded for Fhs/five families; FolD/NrdG versions were not recorded.

Pig-gut homologues came from 617 PiBAC-associated MAGs and isolate genomes. Prodigal 2.6.3 (`-p meta`) predicted proteins; HMMER 3.4 searched gene-specific KEGG/Pfam HMMs (KO `-E 1e-5 --domE 1e-5`; Pfam `--cut_ga`). Score/HMM-coverage cutoffs were: GlyA K00600 ≥84.97/0.70; Fhs K01938 ≥913.80/0.70; FolE K01495 ≥66.33 plus PF01227 gathering cutoff and ≥0.70 model/protein coverage; IscS K04487 ≥264.63/0.70; MetK K00789 ≥70.50/0.70; NrdG K04068 ≥112.47/0.70. FolD required PF00763/PF02882 gathering cutoffs, ≥0.80 coverage per model and 230–350 aa; IscU-like required PF01592 gathering cutoff, ≥0.70 coverage and ≤250 aa. References required `partial=00` and no internal stop. After 70%/80% clustering, up to 40 proteins were selected (one per genome, three per genus). UniProt and eggNOG-derived references were labelled separately.

MAFFT `--auto` and trimAl `-automated1` used 64 threads for Fhs/five families and 16 for FolD/NrdG. MAFFT 7.525 is recorded for the former; trimAl 1.5.rev1 for Fhs. Other run-specific versions were not recorded. IQ-TREE 3.1.2 used `-m MFP` (BIC), `-B 1000 -alrt 1000 --bnni`, and seed 20261025. Trees were unrooted.
