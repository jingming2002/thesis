Figure 1. Overview of auxiliary viral genes (AVGs) in the pig gut virome. (A–B) Counts and CheckAMG confidence composition of metabolic (AMG), physiological (APG), and regulatory (AReG) candidates before and after filtering. (C–D) Viral class and family composition of 5,384 AVG-positive vOTUs. (E–F) Predicted host counts and proportions among host-assigned vOTUs; AVG classes may overlap. Bacillota, Bacillota_A, and Bacillota_C correspond to traditional Firmicutes lineages. (G) Counts of confidently classified virulent (orange) and temperate (blue-green) vOTUs; 524 vOTUs outside the confident lifestyle set are excluded.

Figure 2: Functional composition of porcine enteric auxiliary viral genes in relation to lifestyle. (A) Protein count for 32 functional modules in AMGs, APGs, and AReGs; axes range differently for each category. (B) Percentage of module carriage in predicted virulent (orange; n=6,061) and mild (cyan; n=3,076) vOTUs, along with odds ratios (virulent relative to mild) and precise 95% confidence intervals. Solid markers indicate significance (q<0.05) across all 32 modules after bilateral Fisher's exact test and Benjamini-Hochberg correction. Arrows indicate intervals extending beyond the axis limits; "Inf" indicates an odds ratio of infinity.

Figure 3. Proposed metabolic connections supporting viral DNA synthesis under anaerobic conditions. Folate-mediated one-carbon metabolism feeds purine and thymidylate biosynthesis; Fe–S cluster assembly and SAM synthesis support NrdG-mediated activation of NrdD. Orange, candidate AMG functions identified here; grey, connecting enzymes. Arrows denote links across the repertoire, not co-occurrence within individual genomes.

Figure 4. Phylogenetic relationships and genomic contexts of Fhs, FolD and NrdG. (A, C, E) Midpoint-rooted maximum-likelihood trees of Fhs, FolD and NrdG, respectively. Colours indicate sequence sources; triangles represent collapsed reference clades, with sequence counts in parentheses. Marked nodes indicate strong branch support. (B, D, F) Representative genomic contexts in vOTU_1650, vOTU_17468 and vOTU_8476, respectively. Each overview is linked to an expanded gene neighbourhood. Target genes are purple and viral hallmark genes are blue; arrows indicate coding sequences and transcriptional orientation. Genomic scales vary among panels.
