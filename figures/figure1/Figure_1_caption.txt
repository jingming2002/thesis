Figure 1. Overview of auxiliary viral genes, viral taxonomy, predicted hosts, and viral lifestyles in the pig gut virome. (A) Numbers of auxiliary metabolic genes (AMGs), auxiliary physiological genes (APGs), and auxiliary regulatory genes (AReGs) in the candidate and filtered datasets, coloured by CheckAMG confidence category. (B) Relative proportions of confidence categories within each AVG class before and after filtering. (C-D) Viral class- and family-level composition of the 5,384 AVG-positive viral operational taxonomic units (vOTUs), with unassigned categories retained. (E-F) Numbers and relative proportions of vOTUs assigned to each predicted host phylum, shown for all AVG-positive vOTUs and separately for those carrying AMGs, APGs, or AReGs. Only vOTUs with assigned hosts are included in panels E and F. Individual vOTUs may carry multiple AVG classes and therefore contribute to more than one group. Bacillota, Bacillota_A, and Bacillota_C represent lineages traditionally classified within Firmicutes. (G) Numbers of AVG-positive vOTUs with confident lifestyle predictions: virulent (orange; n = 2,811) and temperate (blue-green; n = 2,049). The remaining 524 AVG-positive vOTUs were not included in the confident lifestyle set and are omitted from panel G. Numerical labels in panels A, E, and G indicate vOTU or gene counts, as appropriate.
