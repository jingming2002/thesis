AVG overview with lifestyle panel, generated entirely in R.
Run: Rscript plot_overview_with_lifestyle.R
Dependencies: ggplot2, scales, svglite (plus base grid).
All plotting TSVs are included. vOTU_class_counts.tsv is the original lifestyle source table with three class rows per vOTU; unique vOTUs carrying any AVG are counted for G.
Total AVG-positive vOTUs: 5384. Confident lifestyle subset: 4860 (2811 Virulent, 2049 Temperate). Remaining 524 are outside this subset.
Panel G has no bottom footnote; scope is documented in the caption.
Virulent = #CC6B2C; Temperate = #2C7F91.
Bacillota, Bacillota_A, Bacillota_C labels retained as in source.
A-F are redrawn from original summary tables without changing their values.
